## What landed

- **Control delivery information** — `app/lib/data/ble/bridge_link.dart`:
  `attemptControl()` returns `BridgeControlAttempt(written, result, error)`.
  `written` is true only after the GATT control write returned, so a later drop
  is the expected outcome of a request the Bridge received (05 §5.12).
  `sendControl()` keeps its old throw-on-failure contract on top of it.
- **Four real flows** — `app/lib/app/bridge_controller.dart`:
  - `forgetPhone()`: local-first. Clears the saved connection immediately, sends
    best-effort `forget_phone`, removes the OS bond on `ok`/disconnect, and sets
    `destructiveNotice` when the pairing could not be confirmed.
  - `factoryReset()`: sends `factory_reset`; `ok` or the expected disconnect is
    success; only then clears the saved connection/bond, keeps phone history and
    marks it orphaned.
  - `powerOff()`: sends `power_off`; on success sets `bridgeOff`, goes offline and
    drops the connected timers, so the app never presents the Bridge as
    reachable. The saved identity stays for a PRG wake.
  - `clearBridgeHistory()`: sends `clear_history`; only `result ok` zeroes the
    displayed Bridge figures. A disconnect is **not** success for this op, and
    the phone copy, pairing and config are untouched.
  - `_attemptDestructive()` maps result → ok/refused, write-then-disconnect →
    `disconnected`, a sent request whose link is gone by timeout →
    `disconnected`, and a live link with no result → `timedOut`.
- **Sheets and outcomes** — `app/lib/features/settings/settings_details.dart`:
  the four confirmation sheets keep the exact 12 §12.2 copy (loss, counts and
  the 1.5 s PRG instruction), the blocking `destructive.sending` dialog runs the
  op, and success/failure is only shown after the outcome. Clear history shows
  `destructive.clear.success` or `destructive.failure`; power off pushes
  `PowerOffScreen`; forget and factory reset return to Setup.
- **Setup return** — `app/lib/features/setup/setup_screen.dart`: saved
  `History on this phone` gains `· from your previous Bridge.` when orphaned and
  the screen shows a `Pairing not confirmed` notice from `destructiveNotice`.
- **Model** — `app/lib/app/view_state.dart`: `DestructiveOutcome.landed`,
  `destructiveNotice`, `historyOrphaned` and `bridgeOff` surfaced through the
  settings/setup view states.

## Facts / gotchas for later tasks

- `BridgeControlAttempt.written` is the only evidence that the Bridge received a
  destructive request. Do not treat a bare `notConnected` before the write as an
  expected disconnect; that is a failure with no local change.
- `clear_history` never treats a disconnect as success (`disconnectIsSuccess:
  false`). Only `factory_reset`, `forget_phone` and `power_off` do.
- Forget is local-first even when the link is down, so it always returns to Setup
  and always keeps phone history; the unconfirmed-pairing notice is the only
  difference in the outcome.
- `clearBridgeHistory()` only zeroes the displayed figures. The new generation
  boundary arrives with the next stats read; T49 firmware owns the epoch change.
- Power off keeps the saved Bridge identity on purpose so a PRG wake can
  reconnect; `bridgeOff` is what stops the UI claiming reachability.
- After a tool timeout, a `flutter test` child can survive and hold the build
  lock; later runs then sit at `loading ...` with no output. Kill stray
  `dart`/`flutter` processes before diagnosing a "hanging" test. Do not pipe
  `flutter test` to `tail` on Windows while diagnosing a hang; it buffers.
- The failed run's `*.log` line in `.gitignore` was reverted; scratch logs were
  deleted. Keep bench evidence under `artifacts/bench/...` tracked.
