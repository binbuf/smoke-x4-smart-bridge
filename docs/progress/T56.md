**Target = probe 4 `max`.** Not `max − 25`, not the midpoint. `min` is `0` in
fan mode. The value is only valid while `billows_attached` is set; detach clears
the flag and restores the non-fan defaults.

## Gotchas for T57/T59

- `max` is the target **only** when `billows_attached` is true. On detach the
  field falls back to `160` and `min` to `32`; reading `max` unconditionally
  would invent a 160 °F target.
- The target followed the base on the next 30 s frame; a target edit does not
  produce an extra packet.
- The first frame after a menu-driven target change had header field 3 = `2`
  and trailing `new_alarm` = `8`, then returned to `1`/`0`. Do not treat that
  edge as target data.
- T56 captured °F only. °C conversion is still T57/T59.
- The Billows fan was left unobstructed and the air probe stayed at room
  temperature; no cook was run.
