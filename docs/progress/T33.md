## What landed

- `app/lib/features/setup/setup_flow.dart`
  - The setup state machine over `BridgeController.bleClient`. It owns
    `SetupStep`, scan rows, connect/`info`, bond outcomes and first-sync
    transition.
  - `findBridge()` checks `platformStatus()` and routes to unsupported,
    Bluetooth off, primer, blocked or scan. `requestPermissionAndScan()` is the
    only caller of `requestPermissions()`.
  - Scan stops at `bleScanTimeout` (10 s), dedupes by device ID, sorts the
    remembered bridge first (device ID then name) and keeps SmokeX4-named
    legacy advertisements as `Requires a newer app` rows.
  - Select connects and reads open `info`; a wrong `proto_major` or
    `bonds_free == 0` stops before `bond()`. `pair()` is the only `bond()` call
    and supplies no passkey.
  - Wrong-code attempts are counted; two failures switch the screen to the
    Android-settings removal instruction.
  - Lab mode: `SetupFlow.frozen(...)` with `initialStep`/`previewBridges`; never
    touches the radio.
- `app/lib/features/setup/setup_screen.dart`
  - Every 13 §13.4 setup state with the approved copy. The product flow has no
    fixed results, no simulated scan/pair timer and no fake OS prompt.
  - Passkey block precedes Pair and shows only the `418 302` example.
  - Pairing progress: `Connecting to the Bridge`, `Checking the code`,
    `Syncing recent history`.
  - First sync: V0 copy `Loading recent readings` and `last 15 minutes`.
  - Saved-history escape on no-Bridge, no-results, unsupported and
    protocol-mismatch screens, plus the orphaned-history screen with readings,
    range, storage and delete.
- `app/lib/data/ble/bridge_codecs.dart`
  - `BridgeInfo` (proto major/minor, device ID, firmware, model, `bonds_free`)
    and `BridgeAdvertisement` (`P4 225°F · 87%`), `scanSignalWord`,
    `scanResultDetail`, `scannedBridgeFor`.
  - Same offsets as `protocol/gen/records.dart`; pinned by
    `test/setup_codec_test.dart` so a contract change fails.
- `app/lib/app/smoke_bridge_app.dart` / `bootstrap.dart`
  - `SmokeBridgeApp` shows `SetupScreen` while `phase == setup` or no saved
    Bridge, otherwise the Live shell.
  - `bootstrap()` creates `FlutterBluePlusBleClient` and starts in setup until
    bridge identity persistence lands.
- `app/lib/app/bridge_controller.dart`
  - `beginSetup()`, `rememberBridge(deviceId:, deviceName:, mtu:)`,
    `rememberedBridgeId`, `rememberedBridgeName`, `negotiatedMtu`.
  - Forget/reset clear the remembered identity but keep local history.

## Gotchas for later tasks

- **T35:** `_afterBond()` in `setup_flow.dart` is a shim. It requests MTU 247,
  records the remembered device and advances to first sync. Replace it with
  `set_time` + recent sync when the procedures land; keep the approved step
  labels.
- **T35/T50:** `rememberedBridgeId`/`rememberedBridgeName` are session-only.
  Persist them and skip setup on later launches when a saved Bridge exists.
- **T35:** call `bond()` only from `SetupFlow.pair()`. The OS owns the passkey
  dialog; `BleClient.bond()` must stay passkey-less in production.
- **Tests:** use `FakeBridge.advertisement(pitTempF10: 2250)` when asserting the
  `P4 225°F` preview; the FakeBridge default P4 temp is 800. Pump exact
  durations while the indeterminate pairing spinner is visible;
  `pumpAndSettle` will time out.
- **Tests:** `FakeBridge` accepts `protoMajor` and `bondsFree` so mismatch and
  full-store states are reachable without a custom GATT server.
- **Settings intents:** `Open Bluetooth settings` / `Open app settings` show a
  neutral instruction. Do not claim a settings screen opened until a platform
  intent exists.
- **Lab:** `SetupScreen(autoAdvance: false)` freezes a state; `previewBridges`
  supplies result rows from `scenario_lab.dart`. Neither parameter belongs in a
  product route.
