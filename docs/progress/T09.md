# T09 — Board identity, bench rig, deep-sleep gate

**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Board:** Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V3, ESP32-S3FN8, 8 MB,
MAC `ac:a7:04:38:82:74` · **Port:** COM5 · **IDF:** 6.0.2

## What landed

- Board identity confirmed without a human: ESP32-S3 rev v0.2, 8 MB GD flash,
  SSD1306 at 0x3C (Vext/RST sequence), SX126x SPI presence, battery divider,
  PRG debounce.
- As-found state archived and decoded: legacy `smoke_bridge` 1.0.0, ESP-IDF
  6.0.2, legacy 7-row OTA partition table, NVS `sx_pair/config` =
  `{918500000, 4c4d58435b5c0000, 4}` + 1 NimBLE bond + Wi-Fi AP state.
- `erase-flash` completed; post-erase flash is all `0xFF` and the board prints
  `invalid header: 0xffffffff` until an image is flashed.
- Bare BLE advertising + deep-sleep probe built at
  `firmware/bench/t09_probe/` and flashed. It advertises `SX4-T09-PROBE` for
  10 s, then deep-sleeps with EXT1 GPIO0-low wake.
- **GPIO0/PRG wake: 20/20 application boots, 0/20 ROM-download entries.**
  Every cycle was `rst:0x5 (DSLEEP)`, `boot:0x8 (SPI_FAST_FLASH_BOOT)`,
  `causes=0x8`, `EXT1 mask=0x1`, `gpio0=0` in `app_main`.
- ADR 0020 records the EXT1 wake decision; 01 §1.4 and 07 §7.4/§7.7 carry the
  bench result; `docs/hardware-verified.md` has every row with a date,
  evidence link and caveat.
- Artifacts: `artifacts/bench/T09/{as-found,wipe,probe}/` with SHA-256 sums.

## Gotchas for later tasks

- **DTR on the CP2102 is the GPIO0/PRG net.** `setDTR(True)` pulls GPIO0 low;
  `setDTR(False)` releases it. This is how the 20 cycles were driven. RTS is
  EN/reset. Opening the port with `dtr=False, rts=False` avoids resetting the
  board on open.
- **SSD1306 needs RST (GPIO21) high before the I2C probe.** A first probe with
  RST left low returned `ESP_ERR_NOT_FOUND`; setting RST high and adding a
  100 ms Vext settle made 0x3C ACK. Vext GPIO36 LOW first, then bus.
- **SX126x version register 0x0320 reads `"SX1261 V2D 2D02"`** on this SX1262
  module — the string is shared across SX1261/1262. Do not treat "1261" in that
  string as a different radio; use GetStatus/board record for presence.
- **IDF 6 does not have `esp_sleep_enable_gpio_wakeup_on_hp_periph_powerdown`
  or `ESP_GPIO_WAKEUP_GPIO_LOW` for ESP32-S3.** Use
  `esp_sleep_enable_ext1_wakeup_io(BIT(0), ESP_EXT1_WAKEUP_ANY_LOW)`; the wake
  cause is `ESP_SLEEP_WAKEUP_EXT1` (bit 3, `causes=0x8`).
- **IDF 6 components are split:** a probe needs `esp_driver_gpio`,
  `esp_driver_i2c`, `esp_driver_spi`, `esp_adc`, `esp_hw_support`,
  `esp_timer`, `hal` in `REQUIRES`, not just `driver`.
- **RTC RAM survives an esptool reset/reflash.** The probe uses a build magic
  (`PROBE_MAGIC`) so a fresh build re-runs the checks; a plain reset after
  flashing can otherwise skip them.
- **The probe waits 10 s with BLE before sleeping.** The task watchdog can fire
  in a bare probe; the probe calls `esp_task_wdt_deinit()`.
- **`sdkconfig`/`build/` under `firmware/bench/t09_probe/` are ignored** by the
  root rules; the probe is outside the product build and not in CI.

## Board state at hand-off

The board runs the T09 probe (3-row `nvs`/`phy_init`/`factory` table) and is
**in deep sleep**, not the product firmware. It is on USB/COM5 with the 3000 mAh
pack connected (≈4.03 V). The legacy image/NVS/cooks are gone by design. T10
must flash its own capture image; nothing may assume the legacy layout or
pairing is present. The product T04 partition table will be written by the next
product flash.

## Follow-ups

- **Physical hold-PRG wake, USB detached, 20/20** — not run: needs a person at
  the bench to press the button on battery. A workable finish:
  1. Keep the pack connected, unplug USB, and press/release PRG for ~2 s twenty
     times with a pause between presses.
  2. Reconnect USB **without removing the pack**, then reset with RTS and read
     `BOOT count=` / `wake_count=`. All 20 app wakes appear as
     `wake_count=20`; a stuck ROM downloader stops the sequence and shows a
     lower count. The probe's RTC counters survive the battery-powered wakes.
  3. Record the result in `docs/hardware-verified.md`, replacing the ⏳.
  The mechanism result is recorded, but the row is still ⏳ until this is done.
- **Current measurements** — on/advertising/OLED-asleep, connected, and deep
  sleep. No current meter in this session. Use a USB/pack inline meter (or an
  INA219) and do not publish the estimate. Replace the three ⏳ rows.
- **24 h pack runtime** — not run; T61/T62 territory.
- **Antenna and pack polarity** — inherited from the legacy record, not
  re-verified. Antenna was in place for the radio probe; any replacement pack
  must be metered before connection.
- **T11/T23** — use ADR 0020: EXT1 on GPIO0 low-level, RTC-retained counter to
  distinguish wake from cold boot, and the 01 §1.4 result. If the physical
  detached repeat ever contradicts this, supersede ADR 0020.
- **T10** — flash the capture image over the probe; the board has no legacy
  NVS or cooks partition now.
