## Findings routed

Defects are recorded here, not fixed (task scope: evidence only).

- **A — T43/T45/T54 (offline semantics):** while offline, probe-tile
  accessibility text still says `updated live` even though the values are
  last-known and dim. `17-cold-launch-absent-13s.xml`.
- **B — T34/T42/T46 (Live sparkline):** after a cold launch the Live tiles show
  `No history yet` although the durable archive has 6 h of readings; the tiles
  use the in-memory recent store, not `SampleDao`. `15-cold-launch-2500.png`,
  `17-cold-launch-absent-13s.png`.
- **C — T43/T45/T54 (fixture leak):** the offline saved-Bridge header shows
  `Bridge A4F2` (the controller default) instead of the saved identity or `—`;
  it returns to `Bridge 8276` after connecting. `17-cold-launch-absent-13s.*`.
- **D — T43/T45 (stuck sync card):** with Bluetooth off the Live screen shows
  the BLOCKED notice *and* `Syncing saved readings` at ~10 %. The card should be
  cleared when the adapter blocks. `18-midsync-bt-off.*`.
- **E — T47 (Bridge stats):** History & storage shows `0 of 0` Bridge readings,
  `Oldest —`, `Newest —` because the 60-byte `stats` values are not exposed on
  the real Bridge yet. `14-history-storage.*`.
- **F — T42/T45 (gap labels):** `HistoryArchive._gapBands` places a stored gap
  band at `detected_ms` (the sync time), not at the missing sequence period, and
  the inferred-hole fallback labels it `No signal`. A `Not synced` row can
  therefore be drawn at the wrong time. The seeded fixture sets `detected_ms` to
  the gap end to place the band correctly. Even then the in-band `Not synced`
  text did not render while the 2-minute/58 px rules applied; the permanent-loss
  banner did. `20-seeded-history-24h.*`, `24-deleted-summary.txt`.
- **G — T42 (Cooks on a full ring):** the seeded 117-day archive produces a
  `Jul 2 2:45 AM – 11:59 PM · 115d 21h` cook with `missing readings`, and
  `Since cook start` stays enabled in a Last-hour range that contains no cook.
  Cooks/Summary sort are archive-wide rather than range-scoped.
  `22-last-hour.xml`, `26-no-cook-sort.xml`.
- **H — T43 (Bridge reboot):** after a Board reboot while the app was running,
  Live read `Bridge Connected` + `Smoke X4 Not paired` with no live values while
  the Board was recording (`stored seq=674`). A force-stop/relaunch recovered.
  `27-real-restored.*`, `serial-main.log` 15:00.
- **I — T54 (320 dp):** truncations at 320 dp: `Smoke ...` in the Live chain,
  `Min … Max 7...`, `24 ho...` / `All pro...`, the P4 legend, and the Storage
  card's `0 on the Brid...`. `29-320-live.png`, `29-320-history.png`,
  `29-320-settings.png`.
- **J — T45 (global loss banner):** the `Some readings were lost…` banner is
  shown for a range that has no loss (Last hour, 23:00 range) because the
  permanent-loss flag is archive-wide. `22-last-hour.*`.
- **K — T53/T54 (local preference persistence):** `Live change window` set to
  1 min at 320 dp was back at 5 min after the app was relaunched; the local
  preference is not persisted across process death. `30-320-live-1min.*`.

## Deviations / blocked

- **30+ minute absence:** shortened to 9.7 min at owner instruction. Forward
  catch-up was proven (one 411 B forward frame, not a re-read); a true 30+ min
  run remains T61.
- **Bridge clear/factory reset:** not implementable on this build; the firmware
  returns `unsupported: not available in this build` for `CLEAR_HISTORY`,
  `FACTORY_RESET`, `POWER_OFF` and `UNPAIR_SMOKE_X`, and the app still
  simulates the clear. T48/T49/T52 own it. Phone history was not deleted by the
  simulated action.
- **24 h sync timing:** not directly measured; recorded as a labelled estimate
  from the real 696-record transfer. The full-ring number is the host fixture.
  A true 24 h and true full-ring radio run belong to T61/T55.
- **X4 amber warning dialog:** the real `No data from Smoke X4` surface was
  observed after the Bridge reboot; the amber paired-lost dialog needs 10 min
  without the X4 and was not re-run. T54/T55.
- The seeded phone fixture is debug-only: it was pushed into the debug app's
  `databases/smoke_bridge.db` and replaced afterwards by the real 696-record
  archive.
