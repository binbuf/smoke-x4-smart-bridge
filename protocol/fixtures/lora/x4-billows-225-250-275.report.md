## Capture path

The product image already logs every accepted state frame as
`sx4.radio: payload="…" hex=…` at INFO level. T56 therefore used the
already-flashed release image and a plain serial capture
(`firmware/bench/t10_probe/tools/capture.py`) instead of flashing the T10
capture probe. Nothing was transmitted: no sync gesture was needed because the
board kept its existing X4 pairing, and the serial capture adds no TX path. The
X4 was not put into SYNC and no pairing ACK was sent during this sitting.

## What was done

1. Captured two detached baseline frames (no Billows).
2. Attached the Billows with the air probe in channel 4, probe at room
   temperature, fan unobstructed.
3. Set the channel-4 target on the **Smoke X4 base** to 250 °F, then 225 °F,
   then 275 °F; four or more state frames were captured at each target.
4. Unplugged the Billows and captured two frames with the flag clear.

The Billows was attached for the whole target sweep. No cook was running. The
receiver was not touched.

## Result

| Base target | Frames | Probe 4 `state` | `alarm` | Probe 4 `max` | Probe 4 `min` | `billows_attached` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| detached | 2 (start) | 0 | 1 | 160 | 32 | 0 |
| 250 °F | 4 | 0 | 1 | 250 | 0 | 1 |
| 225 °F | 4 | 0 | 1 | 225 | 0 | 1 |
| 275 °F | 5 | 0 | 1 | 275 | 0 | 1 |
| detached | 2 (end) | 0 | 1 | 160 | 32 | 0 |

At every target `max` equalled the value shown on the Smoke X4 base, `min`
stayed `0`, and the target followed the base on the next 30 s frame. The
relation is therefore **probe 4 `max` = target**, not `max − 25` and not the
midpoint. `min` does not carry `target − 25` in this capture.

After detach, `billows_attached` cleared to `0` and probe 4's `max`/`min`
returned to the non-fan defaults `160`/`32`. The target must therefore be gated
on `billows_attached`, not read from `max` alone.

## Notes and limits

- Header field 3 moved `1` ↔ `2` and the trailing `new_alarm` field was `8` on
  the first frame after each menu-driven target change, then `0`. That is a
  menu/alarm edge, not target data; the target itself is unambiguous in `max`.
- The target was set to 250 °F first, then 225 °F, then 275 °F. The order does
  not affect the relation; all three targets are present.
- All captures are **°F** (`units=1`). °C conversion is a T57/T59 check, not a
  T56 deliverable.
- The fan was left unobstructed and the probe stayed at room temperature for
  the whole capture.
- RSSI −48…−29 dBm, SNR 8…11 dB. State cadence was the normal ~30 s.
